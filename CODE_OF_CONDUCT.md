# Code of Conduct

We want a useful, safe, and welcoming project for artists, engineers, researchers, and maintainers.

Expected behavior: be respectful, assume good faith, explain technical disagreement with evidence, and protect private assets and credentials.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, malicious payloads, and publishing private material without consent.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the security contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe.
